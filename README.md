# WHO Monthly Measles and Rubella Global Report

This repository provides access to the WHO Monthly Measles and Rubella (MR) Global Reports.

## Monthly Reports

Reports are organized by year:

- [2026 Reports](reports/2026/)
- [2025 Reports](reports/2025/)


## About the Reports
The WHO Monthly Measles and Rubella Reports provide regular updates on measles and rubella surveillance worldwide.
Please check the disclaimer slide in the reports for more information on the underlying data and their limitations.
